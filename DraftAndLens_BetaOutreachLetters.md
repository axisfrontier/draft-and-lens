## 1. Royal Court Theatre
**Contact:** literary@royalcourttheatre.com
**Subject:** A craft reading tool for playwrights — free beta access

Unlike almost every AI writing tool, Draft & Lens does not write, complete, or rewrite your playwrights' work. It reads — the way a thoughtful editor reads. Where it identifies a craft problem, it may sketch a brief invented example to show what it means, the way an editor might mark a margin. It never touches the writer's own words.

I'm a product designer and amateur playwright. I built Draft & Lens because I couldn't find what I actually needed: an editorial reading that understood what my work was trying to do, not a template applied to everything equally.

It identifies the tradition a piece is working in — social realism, absurdist comedy, political theatre — and reads it by those standards. It teaches the craft move, not the fix. Your writers' work stays private and is never used to train AI.

I'm in free beta and looking for serious writers to test it honestly. I wondered whether any writers in your Writers' Group might be willing to put a scene or short play through it and tell me frankly what works and what doesn't. No feedback form, no obligation — just a real reading of a real piece of work.

The site is password protected during beta — reply to this email and I'll send you the access password along with any other details you need.
